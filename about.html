The Artist Behind Sandmagic

Remko Delfgaauw

"When you draw on the sand knowing the sea will claim it within hours, ego falls away. What matters is the purity of the gesture and the presence of the moment."

The Architecture of Impermanence

Remko Delfgaauw has spent nearly three decades exploring the profound dialogue between mathematical sacred geometry, human consciousness, and the elemental forces of the earth.

Beginning in 1997 with hand-drawn geometric crop circles in the Dutch countryside, his work has steadily grown in ambition and scale. In 2009, Remko organized and led the creation of Project Atlas—a 530-meter geometric colossus that earned an official Guinness World Record for the largest land art installation in recorded history.

With a diverse background spanning technology entrepreneurship (as former co-owner of Bever Innovations) and hypnotherapy based in Zierikzee, Remko brings an intense focus on meditative flow, spatial awareness, and non-attachment to every canvas.

A 28-Year Artistic Trajectory
1997

The Golden Gate: First Earth Formations

Remko creates his first monumental earth drawing in the fields of the Netherlands. The realization that celestial geometry could be laid into living grain with wooden boards and ropes sparked a lifelong artistic devotion.

2009

Project Atlas Guinness World Record

A 530-meter masterpiece etched into agricultural fields in Wilhelminadorp. Certified by Guinness World Records as the largest crop circle installation ever constructed, requiring coordinated precision across kilometers of walking lines.

2016

California Heartbeat & Coastal Expansion

Extending his medium from inland fields to the open coastline, Remko travels to the Pacific shores north of San Francisco, creating tidal drawings that interact directly with oceanic surf.

2021

Project Act As One Guinness World Record

Pioneering the genre of "living animation," Remko, René Bosma, and Ron van Jeveren choreograph over one hundred human participants to create kinetic shifting geometry, captured via drone cinematography and awarded his second Guinness World Record.

2023–26

The Sandmagic Era: Coastal Mandalas & The Fire Horse

Focusing on the beaches of Zeeland and the Brouwersdam, Remko creates monumental beach drawings spanning up to 140 meters. In 2026, he unveiled the monumental Fire Horse—a roaring symbol of courage, resilience, and inner fire etched into the sand before being surrendered to the rising tide.

Core Philosophy

"Art that disappears but doesn’t stay silent."

Unlike permanent monuments that demand permanence from a fragile planet, Sandmagic celebrates the beauty of letting go. We borrow the sand during low tide, leave a breathtaking impression for those present, and let the North Sea reclaim every grain.
