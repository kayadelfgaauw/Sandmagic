Artistic Philosophy

"Scale so vast it can only be witnessed from the heavens. Beauty so transient it is surrendered to the sea."

Sandmagic exists at the intersection of monumental sculpture, celestial geometry, and absolute impermanence. We do not quarry stone or pour concrete. Our medium is the damp sand revealed between low and high tide.

Every creation is born in stillness, carved by hand with meticulous mathematical precision, celebrated at its aerial zenith, and returned unconditionally to the ocean. What remains is not physical debris, but an indelible impression on the human psyche.

The Ephemeral Ritual

From Lunar Tide to Pure Sky

Phase 01

Tidal Cartography

Studying lunar tide charts to calculate the exact window of opportunity (typically 4.5 to 6 hours between high tides) and mapping the firmest damp sand.

Phase 02

Sacred Geometry

Translating mathematical designs into physical coordinate stakes, ropes, and compass lines spanning hundreds of meters across the beach.

Phase 03

Physical Raking

Hand-drawn with specialized rakes. Contrast is achieved through the texture of wet subsurface sand against dry surface crystals.

Phase 04

Aerial Zenith

At peak tide delay and optimal solar angle, cine-drones take flight to capture the finished masterwork from altitudes up to 500 meters.

Phase 05

Oceanic Return

The North Sea tide sweeps in. Within twenty minutes, the entire monumental drawing dissolves. The canvas is returned pristine, clean, and silent.

The Artist

Remko Delfgaauw

Land Artist · 2× Guinness World Record Holder · Founder of Sandmagic

"When you draw on the sand knowing the sea will claim it within hours, ego falls away. What matters is the purity of the gesture and the presence of the moment."

After pioneering monumental crop circles in 1997 and orchestrating the historic 530-meter Project Atlas in 2009, Remko expanded his craft onto the shifting sands of the Dutch coast. In 2021, he achieved his second Guinness World Record for Project Act As One.
