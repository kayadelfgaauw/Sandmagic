Artistic Essence

The Architecture of Letting Go

Nearly three decades of dialogue between sacred geometry, human consciousness, and the elemental forces of tide and earth.

Beginning in 1997 with hand-flattened crop formations in the fields of Dreischor, Remko Delfgaauw’s work evolved from nocturnal geometry into world-record monumental earthworks—culminating in the 530-meter Project Atlas (2009) and the kinetic human choreography of Act As One (2021). Today, the vast intertidal sandbanks of Zeeland and Brittany serve as his primary canvas.

Chronological Milestones · 1997—2026

Five Chapters in Earth & Sand

1997

The Golden Gate — First Formation

In the fields of Dreischor, Remko lays his first geometric crop formation using wooden boards and survey rope—discovering the quiet power of drawings visible only from the sky.

2009
Guinness World Record I

Project Atlas — 530 Meters

Orchestrating a dedicated team in Wilhelminadorp, Remko creates the largest crop formation in human history: a 530-by-450-meter winged Vitruvian figure certified by Guinness World Records.

2016

Pacific & North Sea Horizons

Transitioning from inland harvest fields to the open ocean, inscriptions appear along the Sonoma coast north of San Francisco and the wide sandbanks of the Brouwersdam.

2021
Guinness World Record II

Act As One — Living Animation

Together with René Bosma and Ron van Jeveren, over 150 participants become living pixels in a synchronized aerial stop-motion artwork in Zierikzee, earning a second Guinness World Record.

2023–26

The Watersnood Trilogy & The Fire Horse

Monumental commemorative works for the 1953 Flood Museum (1,836 Crosses, A Drop in the Sea, Dientje Kramer) and the 2025–2026 Fire Horse and Brittany mandalas.
