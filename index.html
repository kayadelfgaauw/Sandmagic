The Ephemeral Ritual

Born at Low Tide. Erased by High Water.

Phase I

Lunar Cartography

Reading the tide tables to isolate the four-hour window of firm, damp sand.

Phase II

Sacred Geometry

Anchor stakes and tensioned ropes translate celestial mathematics to coastal scale.

Phase III

The Rake

Hand-raking exposes dark subsurface moisture against sunlit dry crystals.

Phase IV

Aerial Zenith

At peak completion, the work is witnessed from 500 meters above.

Phase V

Oceanic Return

The North Sea advances. Within twenty minutes, the shore is pristine and silent.

The Artist

Remko Delfgaauw

“When you draw on the sand knowing the sea will claim it within hours, ego falls away. What matters is the purity of the gesture and the presence of the moment.”

Two-time Guinness World Record holder (Project Atlas, 2009 & Act As One, 2021). For nearly three decades, Remko Delfgaauw has used the fields and coastlines of Europe and California as an ephemeral canvas.
