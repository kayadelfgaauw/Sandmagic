The Story

The Art of Letting Go

Nearly thirty years of crop circles, living animations, and beach drawings—working with the rhythm of the seasons, the wind, and the tides.

It started in 1997 in the fields of Dreischor, flattening grain by hand with wooden boards and measuring ropes. Over the years, co-creators Remko Delfgaauw and René Bosma turned those night-time crop circles into world-record projects—like the 530-meter Project Atlas (2009) and the living stop-motion animation Act As One (2021)—alongside monumental beach drawings across Zeeland, Brittany, and California.

Highlights Over the Years

1997

The Golden Gate — Where It Began

In the fields of Dreischor, Remko makes his first crop circle using wooden boards and rope—discovering the magic of drawings you can only really see from the sky.

2009
Guinness World Record

Project Atlas — 530 Meters

Together with a dedicated team in Wilhelminadorp, Remko creates the largest crop figure ever made: a 530-by-450-meter butterfly man, officially recognized by Guinness World Records.

2016

From Fields to the Ocean

Trading harvest fields for the coast, drawings appear along the Pacific beach north of San Francisco and on the wide sandbanks of the Brouwersdam.

2021
Guinness World Record

Act As One — Living Animation

Together with René Bosma and Ron van Jeveren, more than 150 people form a living stop-motion animation filmed from above in Zierikzee—earning a second Guinness World Record.

2023–26

Watersnood Commemorations & The Fire Horse

Large beach drawings for the 1953 Flood Museum (1,836 Crosses, A Drop in the Sea, Dientje Kramer), followed by new works in Brittany and The Fire Horse.
